TRADEVELOCITY 0.3.3 - WINDOWS APP

Prepared for local testing before publication. No real-money trading.

OPEN THE APP
1. Extract the entire ZIP to a folder you can write to, for example Documents.
2. Open Launch_Desktop.vbs, or TradeVelocity\TradeVelocity.exe directly.
3. Keep the TradeVelocity folder and its _internal folder together.
   Do not launch from inside the ZIP or copy only the EXE.

You do not need a separate Python or Node.js installation.
Windows 10/11 x64, WebView2 Evergreen and .NET Framework 4.6.2+ are required.
If startup reports a missing prerequisite, read WINDOWS-README.md for official
setup links. Nothing is installed automatically. The EXE is unsigned; Windows
may show a warning. Do not disable antivirus or system security protections.

TEST IT WITHOUT CLEARING EXISTING RECORDS
1. Check that Overview opens and the light/dark theme button works.
2. In Orders & depth, enter a new custom engine symbol such as TVCHECK.
   Choose a different symbol if that one already has orders or trades.
3. Submit a SELL limit order: 5 shares, price 100. It should remain open.
4. Submit a BUY limit order: 3 shares, price 100. It should fill 3 shares,
   leave 2 shares on the sell order, and record a trade at price 100.
5. Close and reopen the app. Select your test symbol again; check that the
   remaining order and trade were saved. Desktop/browser sessions are separate.
6. Amend the remaining sell order to 4 shares at price 101. Then cancel that
   test order. The execution already recorded must remain in Trade history.
7. Export session and Export all trades from Trade history. Keep the files.
   Do not clear/import over your normal records just to test restoration.
8. Check Market history and chart controls. Provider refresh needs internet;
   cached history is not a live exchange feed. Custom symbols have no provider
   history. A small matching test leaves AI warming up until 40 executed-order
   observations; that is expected, not a failure.
9. Close the window. Your saved data should remain, and the app should stop.
